01 / Institutional oversight and accountability

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors provides governance oversight, protects SADGF's independence and public-interest mandate, and holds executive leadership accountable for the organisation's direction and stewardship.

Provides oversight of the Executive Board.

Responsibilities

  • Oversee institutional strategy and governance
  • Support responsible stewardship and management of institutional risks
  • Maintain accountability for the organisation's mandate

02 / Leadership and implementation

Executive Board

The Executive Board leads the implementation of SADGF's work, coordinating research, publications, convening and institutional engagement.

Leads implementation within the organisation's governance arrangements and coordinates the work of subcommittees.

Responsibilities

  • Translate institutional priorities into programmes and activities
  • Coordinate research, publications and stakeholder relationships
  • Report progress and operational matters to the Board of Directors

03 / Focused work and delivery

Subcommittees

Subcommittees support defined areas of SADGF's work through focused research, planning and implementation.

Work within the mandate delegated to them and report through the appropriate Board structure.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out work within agreed terms of reference
  • Bring relevant expertise to defined projects
  • Report activities and recommendations through the responsible governance structure

Public trust

Our commitment to accountability

SADGF's institutional principles are independence, transparency, evidence-informed reasoning, inclusion and the public interest. Governance responsibilities are distinct from advisory input and operational delivery.

For questions about governance arrangements, appointments or institutional documentation, contact SADGF.
