Big questions. Real-world impact.

Technology moves fast.
People come first.

From the algorithms shaping our opportunities to the data we share every day, digital governance affects all of us.

SADGF is an independent, non-partisan forum connecting research, public policy and lived experience. Human rights, accountability, jobs and livelihoods are at the heart of our work.

01 / AI governance

Who gets a say when AI makes a decision?

From job applications to public services, automated decisions can shape our opportunities. We explore the transparency, human oversight and accountability people need.

02 / Data governance

Your data tells a story. Who gets to use it?

Every digital interaction leaves a trace. Responsible data governance means protecting people's rights and asking how information is collected, shared and used.

  • Privacy
  • Data stewardship
  • Digital identity

03 / Digital public infrastructure

What if digital opportunity reached everyone?

Useful digital services should be accessible, trusted and inclusive. We examine the infrastructure and public policy needed to make participation possible across South Africa.

  • Public services
