Privacy notice

How information submitted through the SADGF website is used, and how to contact us about it.

People. Policy. Possibility.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who to contact

The South African Digital Governance Forum operates this website. Contact info@sadgf.org.za about personal information, access or correction requests, or to withdraw a publication subscription.

Information you choose to provide

Our enquiry form asks for your name, email address, enquiry type and message. Your organisation is optional. The publication-updates form asks for your email address and express permission to send updates; your name is optional. You can browse our public content without submitting a form.

Why we collect it

Enquiry details are used to respond to and manage your request. Publication subscription details are used to manage the updates you requested. Please only provide information relevant to your enquiry, and avoid sending sensitive personal information through these forms.

Website and form services

The website and its forms use Netlify. When you submit a form, the information is processed through that service so it can be made available to SADGF. Hosting services may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and protect the website. Service-provider processing may take place outside South Africa.

Fonts and images are served with this website. We do not embed a map or install advertising or analytics cookies in this version of the site. External links, including social platforms, have their own privacy practices.

Your choices and requests

You may ask SADGF to access, correct or delete information you have supplied, or object to its use where applicable. You can withdraw permission for publication updates by emailing us with “Unsubscribe” in the subject or using the privacy enquiry option. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Retention enquiries

For details of the retention period applicable to your enquiry or subscription, or to request deletion, contact SADGF at the address above. Do not assume that submitting an enquiry also subscribes you to publication updates.

Concerns

Please contact SADGF about a privacy concern. You can also contact South Africa's Information Regulator about the protection of personal information.
