# Publication images

These are local WebP versions of photographs and supplied graphics selected for the nine publication topics. Resizing and compression are the only asset changes. Supplied graphics use `fit: contain` to preserve their embedded text and content in every placement.

The complete source register is `src/_data/publicationPhotography.json`: image titles, available creator credits, source pages, download URLs (or supplied local assets), licences, accessible descriptions, dimensions and responsive variants. Public attribution is rendered at `photo-credits.html`, linked beside every use and from the footer. Images supplied on 30 October 2026 are preserved in `supplied/`; their creators are not attributed to SADGF and no external licence has been assumed.

Creative Commons derivatives in this folder retain the licence recorded for the original photograph. Supplied images, including the summit photographs, are provided for this website; this folder does not grant a licence for third-party reuse of those images.

For replacements, download the source identified in the register and use Sharp to rotate according to EXIF, resize without enlargement to widths 640 and 1600, and encode WebP at quality 80. Preserve the aspect ratio and use the original width when it is smaller. Update the register and `publications.json` together. The article feature, homepage and archive share the same publication image reference.
