Independent thinking. Public purpose.

We bring law, public policy and technical expertise together to strengthen accountability in South Africa's digital future.

People. Policy. Possibility.

Digital governance that puts people first

SADGF is an independent, non-partisan forum working at the intersection of AI governance, law, public policy, digital transformation and the public interest.

We bring policymakers, researchers, technology practitioners, civil society and young people into a shared conversation about how digital systems should be governed.

Our work centres on human rights, institutional accountability and the effects of AI on jobs and livelihoods. Digital progress should expand participation and opportunity while giving people meaningful ways to understand and challenge decisions that affect them.

Our direction

Trust, participation and accountability

Our vision

A South African digital future in which innovation strengthens public trust, respects human rights and expands opportunity.

Our mission

To advance responsible oversight and informed public participation through dialogue, accessible research and non-binding guidance.

Our approach

Independent, evidence-informed engagement that connects technical understanding with the lived experiences of people and communities.

Strategic priorities

From ideas to public value

Convene

Create spaces for cross-sector dialogue about AI, data and digital governance.

Publish

Translate research and technical questions into accessible policy briefs, discussion papers and reports.

Advise

Contribute non-binding guidance that supports accountable decisions in public and private institutions.
